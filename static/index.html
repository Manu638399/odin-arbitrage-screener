Backtester

Backtest static spread levels on the loaded series. Buy side: goes long the spread (open < Buy Entry; close > Buy Exit); Sell side: goes short (open > Sell Entry; close < Sell Exit). Leave a side blank to disable it. Cost is charged per side (entry and exit). PnL is in spread price units.

Estimate cost / side

Approximate the per-side cost from venue fees and the MT5 spread, then apply it to Cost / side. The HyperLiquid fee is % of notional (converted via the reference price, auto-filled from the loaded pair); the MT5 fee is direct cost in price units; the MT5 spread is auto-filled from the live MT5 order book — bid and charged as a half-spread per side. All in spread price units.
